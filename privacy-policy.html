ContextDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-02

ContextDrop is a browser extension that captures page content you choose and formats it as AI prompt packs.

Data we process

  • On-device: page title, URL, selected text, extracted main content, prompt history, and settings stored in chrome.storage.local.
  • Optional account/Pro: email and license status if you purchase Pro/Team; synced packs if you enable cloud sync.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not capture browsing history in the background.
  • Free local mode does not upload page content to ContextDrop servers.

Third parties

When you click Send, content may be pasted or injected into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini under those providers’ terms. Payment processing may use Stripe.
